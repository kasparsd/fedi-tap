Enter your Fediverse account to set up an Apple Shortcut for liking, reposting and replying to content on the Fediverse from your own Fediverse server, such as a WordPress site with the ActivityPub plugin.

Step 1: Download Shortcut

Download Shortcut (View source on GitHub)

Step 2: Open the downloaded file in Apple Shortcuts

Choose Add Shortcut.

Step 3: Authorize Shortcut

Sign-in requires JavaScript and the published HTTPS page.

Sign in again to get fresh shortcut settings.

Troubleshoot CORS

Step 4: Connect Shortcut

After you sign in to your Fediverse server, choose Connect FediTap to pass your tokens to the shortcut. This page holds the tokens only in memory. Your browser remembers your Fediverse handle, and the shortcut saves the tokens in iCloud Drive.
