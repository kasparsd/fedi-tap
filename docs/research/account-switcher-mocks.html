Account switcher: avatar mocks

Exploration for the account switcher; the decisions that hold are in components.md. Tap a circle to switch, or the current account or “+N” to open the accounts sheet.

Signed out

The name shows only until there is an account.

Header

Up to three other accounts are circles. Beyond that, two circles and “+N”. Switching puts the previous account where the tapped one was, so switching back is the same spot. With large text the circles move to a second line.

Telling accounts apart

Each account gets the least-used colour when it is added and keeps it. Accounts with the same initial also show a second letter.

Photos

An account’s own photo replaces its letters. Letters show only when there is no photo or it fails to load. The photos here are drawn placeholders.

States

The sheet

Every account in full, one tap to switch, Remove on each row, and the field to add another. It opens with focus on the current account and closes with Done, or the “×” at the top right.
